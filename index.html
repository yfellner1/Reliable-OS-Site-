FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Don’t see your question? Give us a call at 732.226.8111.

Is Reliable Office Services a call center?

Not at all! All our staff are in our Lakewood, NJ office, and we assign a personal rep to your account. We want you to build a personal relationship with the person you work with, and we’d love for you to come down and meet them!

Is any of the work done overseas?

No. Every member of our team works from our Lakewood, NJ office. Nothing is outsourced overseas.

Is there any commitment on my end?

None at all! Every plan is month to month. If the service isn’t working for you, you can cancel at any time. We just ask that you give us 30 days’ notice.

What if my customer wants to speak with me directly?

No problem! We collect their name, number and reason for the callback, so you’re fully informed and can make the most of your time.

Are you able to use my software?

Absolutely! We learn your business and follow your preferences. We only provide our own systems if you don’t already have one you like.

Will customers lose the personal service they’re used to with me?

In our experience, customers appreciate someone picking up their calls and are happier getting through to someone, even if it isn’t you. They can still reach you, but in a more organized way. Scheduling a call with the details ready makes the whole process more effective and efficient.

How will my receptionist know how to get the work done?

We have an extensive onboarding process to learn your business. We document your preferences and former methods, then put them into practice on our end.
